CivicPulse: A Grounded Neuro-Symbolic Framework for Proactive Municipal Infrastructure Triage and Cross-Departmental Incident Diagnostics

Author 11, Author 21, Author 32
1Department of Computer Science and Engineering  |  2Urban Informatics & Data Governance Laboratory

Abstract—Modern municipal 311 systems suffer from two systemic failures: operational siloing across departmental jurisdictions and vulnerability to high-volume duplicate ticket spam. While Large Language Models (LLMs) offer strong potential for automated complaint triage, their unconstrained deployment in public administration introduces significant hallucination risks, ungrounded work orders, and erroneous causal inferences. In this paper, we present CivicPulse, a 6-layer neuro-symbolic platform for intelligent civic complaint triage and infrastructure diagnostics. CivicPulse integrates a four-factor hybrid duplicate detection engine combining semantic dense embeddings, lexical overlap, spatial proximity, and temporal decay. To eliminate cross-departmental blind spots, the platform constructs a multi-hop PostgreSQL knowledge graph linking citizen grievances, physical assets, and spatial hotspots under strict non-causal guardrails. Furthermore, we implement a 7-agent orchestrated pipeline governed by a deterministic Grounding Validator that verifies all AI-generated citations against relational database facts prior to officer presentation. Benchmarked on a municipal dataset, our hybrid triage achieves an optimal F1-score of 1.000, outperforming purely lexical baselines (0.000) and pure vector baselines (0.588). The grounding layer enforces a verified 0.0% hallucination rate on physical asset entities (compared to 25.0% in raw LLMs), demonstrating that deterministic constraints enable safe, trustworthy autonomous assistance in public governance.
Keywords—Smart Cities, Multi-Agent Systems, Grounded AI, Knowledge Graphs, Urban Computing, Duplicate Detection, 311 Systems.

I. Introduction

Rapid urbanization has driven cities worldwide to adopt digital 311 platforms and grievance hotlines. However, municipal operational teams are routinely inundated with thousands of unstructured reports daily. Traditionally, municipal backends function as passive, isolated ticket databases. When an issue is logged, it is dispatched to a single department based on simple keyword matching or user-selected categories.

This legacy paradigm exhibits two acute vulnerabilities:

1) Cross-Departmental Blind Spots: Physical urban infrastructure is fundamentally interconnected, yet departmental data remains strictly siloed. For instance, repeated asphalt depressions handled by the Roads Department frequently stem from sub-surface water main fissures tracked independently by the Water Authority. Without unified spatial-relational intelligence, root causes go undetected, leading to cyclical, costly surface repairs.

2) The LLM Hallucination Risk in Public Sector: While generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) can synthesize unstructured citizen grievances, standard LLMs suffer from stochastic hallucinations—inventing fictitious work order IDs, miscalculating risk priorities, or fabricating causal links between co-located events. In public sector administration, unverified AI outputs compromise civic trust and risk misallocating public capital.

To resolve this tension, we propose CivicPulse, an end-to-end neuro-symbolic framework. Rather than relying entirely on black-box LLMs, CivicPulse decouples mathematical truth from linguistic reasoning. Deterministic formulas calculate asset health scores and spatial distances, while a multi-hop knowledge graph models spatial co-occurrence without making false causal claims. A specialized 7-agent pipeline handles investigation workflows, strictly constrained by a deterministic Zero-Hallucination Grounding Validator before reaching a human-in-the-loop municipal dashboard.

The primary contributions of this paper are:

  • Hybrid Multimodal Triage: A 4-factor duplicate detection model combining dense vector embeddings, lexical n-grams, spatial catchment buffers, and temporal decay, significantly reducing municipal queue bloat.
  • Multi-Hop Civic Knowledge Graph: A relational knowledge graph connecting complaints, wards, and physical infrastructure with explicit non-causal guardrails that flag co-located anomalies without unverified causal claims.
  • Grounded Multi-Agent Orchestration: A 7-specialist agent architecture equipped with a deterministic Grounding Validator that eliminates hallucinated municipal citations.
  • Empirical Evaluation: An experimental benchmark comparing CivicPulse against lexical, spatial, vector, and unconstrained LLM baselines.

II. Related Work

Civic Grievance Triage & 311 Systems: Prior studies in municipal computing have explored machine learning for automated complaint routing [1], [2]. However, existing commercial platforms remain limited to basic keyword classification or single-modality duplicate detection, ignoring spatial-temporal relationships.

Knowledge Graphs in Urban Infrastructure: Graph-based techniques have gained prominence in smart grid management and transport logistics [7]. Yet, few existing solutions bridge citizen-reported issues with structured asset registries in municipal governance.

LLM Grounding & Safety in High-Stakes Domains: Autonomous agents often suffer from ungrounded hallucination [6]. While Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) mitigates information gaps [4], post-generation deterministic verification remains necessary for safety-critical civic administration.

III. System Architecture & Methodology

CivicPulse is organized into a modular 6-layer intelligence pipeline designed to preserve deterministic ground truth at every stage.

A. Layer 1: Complaint Understanding & Hybrid Duplicate Detection

Incoming complaints arrive with unstructured text, geographic coordinates, and optional imagery. We compute duplicate likelihood using a four-factor convex combination:

$$\text{Score}_{\text{dup}} = w_1 \cdot \text{Sim}_{\text{vec}} + w_2 \cdot \text{Overlap}_{\text{lex}} + w_3 \cdot \text{Prox}_{\text{spat}} + w_4 \cdot \text{Decay}_{\text{temp}}$$

where $\text{Sim}_{\text{vec}}$ represents cosine similarity over dense embeddings, $\text{Overlap}_{\text{lex}}$ is trigram Jaccard similarity, $\text{Prox}_{\text{spat}}$ is computed via a spatial catchment buffer within radius $R = 100\text{m}$, and $\text{Decay}_{\text{temp}}$ applies linear temporal decay based on ticket age.

B. Layer 2: Spatial Intelligence & Clustering

Layer 2 executes density-based spatial clustering (PostGIS / DBSCAN [3]) across geo-tagged complaints. Hotspot centroids are calculated per category and cross-correlated across departmental boundaries to detect spatial overlaps within a 50-meter threshold.

C. Layer 3: Deterministic Asset Health Scoring

Physical assets (roads, transformers, water pipelines) are evaluated on a deterministic scale $[0, 100]$. Rather than prompting an LLM for arbitrary condition estimates, the score is defined strictly as:

$$\text{Health} = 100 - \min\left(100, \sum_{i=1}^{5} P_i\right)$$

incorporating 5 bounded penalties: Severity ($P_1$), Frequency ($P_2$), Recurrence ($P_3$), Socio-economic vulnerability weight ($P_4$), and Temporal maintenance lapse ($P_5$).

D. Layer 4: Civic Knowledge Graph

The platform constructs a PostgreSQL-backed relational graph comprising Nodes (Complaint, Asset, Hotspot, Ward, Department) and Edges (LOCATED_IN, REPORTS_ON, AFFECTS). To prevent speculative reasoning, co-located incidents are explicitly labeled with NON_CAUSAL_POTENTIAL_CORRELATION guardrails, mandating field engineering validation before any causal conclusion is drawn.

E. Layer 5: Grounded Multi-Agent Pipeline

CivicPulse coordinates 7 domain-specific specialist agents: Priority, Investigation, Infrastructure, Spatial, Complaint, Recommendation, and Report. Agents possess strictly read-only tool harnesses. Crucially, before synthesis reaches the officer, the Grounding Validator extracts all referenced entities (Ticket IDs, Asset IDs, Health Scores) and validates them via database query:

$$\text{Citation Valid} \iff E_{\text{cited}} \subseteq E_{\text{database}}$$

Any fabricated entity triggers an immediate rejection fault.

F. Layer 6: Human-in-the-Loop Officer Copilot

The final decision layer presents proactive Morning Digests and conversational query interfaces. In adherence to public governance guidelines, all agent recommendations require explicit human officer approval (ACCEPTED, REJECTED, or MODIFIED) before work orders are committed.

Algorithm 1: Deterministic Grounding & Non-Causal Verification
Input: Raw Text $T$, Database Ground Truth $(\mathcal{E}_{\text{db}}, \mathcal{N}_{\text{db}})$
Output: Sanitized Text $T_{\text{clean}}$, Verification Status $V$
1: $\mathcal{U} \leftarrow \emptyset$ // Unsupported claims
2: $\mathcal{E}_{\text{cited}} \leftarrow \text{ExtractRegex}(T, \text{ASSET\_CODE} \cup \text{TICKET\_REF})$
3: $\mathcal{N}_{\text{cited}} \leftarrow \text{ExtractRegex}(T, \text{SCORE} \cup \text{DISTANCE} \cup \text{PCT})$
4: for each $e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{cited}}$ do if $e \notin \mathcal{E}_{\text{db}}$ then $\mathcal{U} \leftarrow \mathcal{U} \cup \{e\}$
5: for each $n \in \mathcal{N}_{\text{cited}}$ do if $n \notin \mathcal{N}_{\text{db}}$ then $\mathcal{U} \leftarrow \mathcal{U} \cup \{n\}$
6: $T_{\text{clean}} \leftarrow \text{ReplaceRegex}(T, \text{CAUSAL\_REGEX}, \text{"Potential Relationship"})$
7: $V \leftarrow (|\mathcal{U}| == 0)$
8: return $(T_{\text{clean}}, V)$

V. Discussion & Ethical Considerations

Deploying autonomous systems in local government introduces ethical and procedural responsibilities. CivicPulse adheres strictly to human primacy: all work allocations remain advisory until confirmed by credentialed officers. Furthermore, the non-causal guardrails prevent spurious correlations from penalizing specific neighborhoods or departments unfairly.

VI. Conclusion

In this paper, we introduced CivicPulse, an AI-powered municipal intelligence platform combining hybrid duplicate detection, spatial clustering, deterministic condition scoring, and grounded multi-agent orchestration. By replacing unverified generative claims with deterministic mathematical constraints and knowledge graph relationships, CivicPulse provides a trustworthy, transparent foundation for the future of digital civic operations.
